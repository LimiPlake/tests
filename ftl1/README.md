# LimiMake Level 1 Final Test

This folder contains the complete Level 1 final-test front end for the `ftl1/` folder in the `tests` repository.

It follows the wireframe requirements captured in the project handoff:

- one wireframe section on screen at a time, with its questions arranged in the wireframe's rows;
- 5 single-choice questions, 4 multi-select questions, 3 written answers, 6 true/false questions, and 1 final-project screen;
- 50% or higher is the stated passing threshold;
- two hours of working time;
- three five-minute pauses;
- local autosave and restoration after a refresh;
- human-only review, with no answer key or automatic grading in the browser.

The final project instructions tell the student to use every Level 1 concept, export `FinalProject.sjr`, and email it to `questions@limiplake.com`.
